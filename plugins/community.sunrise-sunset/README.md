# Sunrise and sunset

Shows today's sunrise and sunset times for a place.

Settings:
- Latitude and Longitude: the place, in decimal degrees (default Melbourne: -37.8136, 144.9631). South and west are negative.
- Time zone: the place's time zone, like Europe/London or America/New_York, so the times are local to it.

Notes:
- Data: sunrise-sunset.org. No account or key.
- Checks every 3 hours.

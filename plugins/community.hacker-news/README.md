# Hacker News

Shows new stories from the Hacker News front page, one per card, as they appear. Long titles continue on a second card.

- Data: hnrss.org (an unofficial, free RSS service for Hacker News). No account or key.
- Checks for new stories every 15 minutes.
- Tip: add alert words in the source's settings (for example "outage") to have matching stories interrupt the rotation.

# Game of Life

Conway's Game of Life on your board, a generation every 10 seconds. A cell with two or three live neighbours lives on, an empty one with exactly three comes alive, and the rest die; the edges wrap round. Cells are coloured by age, so you can watch new ones (yellow) appear and long-lived ones turn green, blue and then violet.

When everything dies, or it settles into a loop, it starts again from a random soup or a famous pattern: a glider, the R-pentomino, the acorn, diehard, a spaceship or a pulsar.

Settings:
- Colour: by age, or one colour.

Notes:
- Nothing is fetched: it runs on your device.
- This is a script plugin (Rattleboard 0.8.0 or later) with no network access at all.

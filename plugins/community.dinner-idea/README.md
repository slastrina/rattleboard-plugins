# Dinner idea

Can't decide what to make? A random meal (or cocktail), with its cuisine and kind, new every time it comes round in the rotation (Rattleboard 0.10.7 and later; earlier versions get a new one every hour). Click the board to open the recipe.

Settings:
- Suggest: meals, cocktails, or both at random.

Notes:
- Data: [TheMealDB](https://www.themealdb.com) and [TheCocktailDB](https://www.thecocktaildb.com), free with their public test key, no account needed. Please keep it to one source of these per board.
- This is a script plugin (Rattleboard 0.8.0 or later). It only contacts www.themealdb.com and www.thecocktaildb.com.

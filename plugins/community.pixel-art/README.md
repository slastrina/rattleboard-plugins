# Pixel art

Little pictures made of colour tiles: a heart, a space invader, a chomping yellow circle chasing a ghost, a mushroom, a tree, a house, a smiley and a cat, plus a rainbow, a sunset and a night sky drawn to fill any size of board. A different one every time it comes round in the rotation (Rattleboard 0.10.7 and later; earlier versions change every hour).

Notes:
- Compact boards get smaller versions.
- Nothing is fetched: the pictures are drawn on your device.
- This is a script plugin (Rattleboard 0.8.0 or later) with no network access at all.

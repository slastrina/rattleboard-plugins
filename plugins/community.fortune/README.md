# Fortune teller

A fortune-cookie fortune with four lucky numbers, or the crystal ball's answer to whatever you were wondering (green for yes, yellow for maybe, red for no). New every time it comes round in the rotation (Rattleboard 0.10.7 and later; earlier versions get a new one every hour).

Settings:
- Show: fortunes and the crystal ball, fortune cookies only, or the crystal ball only.

Notes:
- Nothing is fetched: the fortunes are built in and picked on your device.
- This is a script plugin (Rattleboard 0.8.0 or later) with no network access at all.

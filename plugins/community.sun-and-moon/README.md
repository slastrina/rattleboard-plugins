# Sun and moon

Shows today's sunrise and sunset where you are, the moon's phase and how much of it is lit. The corner colour follows the moon: violet at new moon, blue for a crescent, green at a quarter, yellow when gibbous and white at full moon.

Settings:
- Latitude and Longitude: where you are, in degrees (south and west are negative). The defaults are Melbourne.
- Times: 12-hour or 24-hour.

Notes:
- Nothing is fetched: the sunrise equation and the moon's age are worked out on your device, every 5 minutes. Times are within a minute or two of an almanac's.
- Near the poles it says when the sun stays up or down all day.
- This is a script plugin (Rattleboard 0.8.0 or later) with no network access at all.
